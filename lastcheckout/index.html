STAFF CARDTILL PROCEDURE · PLEASE READ
  1. Scan: drag each thing over the scanner glass. The barcode is always on the back, so tap a thing to turn it over first.
  2. Weigh: loose fruit and veg has no barcode. Put it on the glass, then pick what it is on the till.
  3. Bag: drop it in the bag. Things stack up, so heavy at the bottom: tins and milk will squash eggs, crisps, bread and flowers.
  4. Park: the counter holds things while you wait to bag them. When a bag's full, hand it over and start another.
  5. Total: when it's all in a bag, press TOTAL. Cards pay themselves. For cash, tap coins and notes to make up the change, then GIVE.
  6. Some customers will ask for things. There's no wrong answer. It's the end of the world.
