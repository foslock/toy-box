Midnight
Golf

Nine holes of mini golf through a sleeping house. Every putt makes a noise. Don't wake anyone.

Pick a hole

Holes open up as you reach them.

Shh. Paused.

How to play

Putt: drag back from the ball, like a catapult, and let go. Further back is harder.

Noise: putts, bumps and clattery floors all make noise. Each sleeper has a ring that fills as they hear things. A full ring and they're awake, and you start that hole again.

Wait: rings drain when it goes quiet. The pale wedge while you aim is what this putt alone will add.

Moons: sink it without anyone's ring passing the halfway tick and you get a moon .

Keys: ← → aim · ↑ ↓ power · space putt · R restart · Esc menu
