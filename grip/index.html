Gannet HeadRoute 14

Grip

HVS 5awith weather

One long line up the sea cliff, the old lighthouse and the radio mast on its dome. There are no belays and no checkpoints. Every fall ends wherever you land.

Pitches

  1. The Sea Cliff, 30 m. Jugs, then crimps, a crumbling band, a lunge and an old rope.
  2. The Lighthouse, 23 m. The keeper's ladder, missing several rungs.
  3. The Mast, 27 m. Steel, rust and wind off the sea.

Technique

    Your high point is kept between visits. Sound on is best.
