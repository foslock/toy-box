New recruits report here

Four short panels: aim, dodge, lead your target, and drop a mortar where the enemy will be. Takes about two minutes.

The rules in brief

  1. Each round, one soldier per side gets to walk a few steps and fire.
  2. You both plan in secret, then it all happens at once.
  3. So aim where they'll be, and don't stay where they're aiming.
  4. Last side standing wins. After round 10, the tide comes in.

Versus the computer

Three generals have agreed to fight, each with habits you can learn to read.

Two generals, one phone

Pass and play. Red plans, hands over the phone, then Ink plans without peeking. Then everybody watches.

Controls. Drag back from your soldier to aim, like a slingshot. Tap the ground to walk there. On a keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, A D walk, 1–4 weapons, Enter to lock in.
