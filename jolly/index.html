Jolly Cooperator

a shadow play in three acts

Somebody in trouble has touched your summoning sign. You are a spirit cut from the lamp's own light, sent to see them through three acts. You can fight, but you cannot speak: only point, beckon, wait, bow and spin. Keep your host standing. If they fall, you go home.

Whose sign will you answer?
