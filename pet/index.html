    Grown-ups you've raised

    How to play

    Press A to move through the icons, B to use one, C to go back. On a keyboard: A B C, or ← Enter Esc.

    Food
    A meal fills a hunger heart. A snack fills a happy heart but piles on weight.
    Light
    Switch it off when your pet falls asleep, or it sleeps badly.
    Game
    Guess which way it will look. Win three of five for a happy heart.
    Medicine
    When a skull shows, it's sick. Some bugs take two doses.
    Bathroom
    Flush the mess away. Left too long, it makes your pet sick.
    Meter
    Age, weight, discipline and hearts.
    Discipline
    If it calls you with full hearts, or turns down a meal, it's being naughty: scold it. Scolding a good pet upsets it.

    A call you leave unanswered for half an hour counts as a care mistake, and mistakes (plus discipline) decide which grown-up it becomes. It hatches in a minute, is a baby for an hour, a child for about a day and a teen for another. It sleeps at night and needs you every few hours by day.

    It keeps living while this page is closed. Come back and it will have been hungry, messy or asleep without you. It only dies after a full day with no care at all. Everything is kept in this browser.
