      Hold your horse

      The ring steward has stopped the clock.

      How to work a horse

      1. One thumb each end. Left thumbA is the back end, right thumbL the front end. Every tap is one step.
      2. Keep them in step: back, front, back, front. Let one end run off and the costume stretches and rips. Let them bunch up and the back headbutts the front.
      3. Hold, then let go, to hop. Hop the front end first. The back end can’t see, so it hops when the front shouts “Ready… Hup!”
      4. Don’t mash. Tap while a hoof is still in the air and you trip over your own feet.
      5. Go over and you start again from the last flag. The clock keeps running.
