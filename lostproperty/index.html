HOW THE LEDGER WORKS

Forty years of things the people of Gullhaven lost, and the clerks who wrote them down.

> Type any word. The terminal shows the first five entries that contain it, oldest first, and how many more there are.

> Two words find entries with both. Open an entry and tap any word in it to search for that word.

> Pin what matters to your pad and jot notes there. Everything is kept for next time.

> Somebody kept handing things in on purpose. When you know who, and who it was all for, fill in the WHO? card on the pad.
