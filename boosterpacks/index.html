How to play

Open a pack: swipe across the dotted line at the top to tear it, pull the cards out, then swipe or tap through them. The rare is always last. Special cards arrive face down and glowing: tap to flip them.

Every card has a value. Commons are worth up to $1 and uncommons up to $10, and most are worth a lot less. A rare can be worth anything; the dearest are the hardest to find. 1 card in 40 is Holo (5×), 1 in 20 is Full Art (2×), and a card that's both is worth 50×.

Look closer: tap a card after a pack or in your binder. Drag it, or on a phone just tilt the phone, to catch the light on its foil, and tap outside it to put it back.

Sell cards after a pack, or any time from your binder, to buy more packs. Once you could afford ten packs, booster boxes go on sale: ten packs opened at once.

Share a pull: tap a card for a closer look, then Share to send a link to it. Whoever opens the link can admire the card, but it stays yours: it isn't added to their binder, and they can't sell it.

Expansions: once your binder holds 50 different Around the House cards, the Backyard set unlocks, with packs of its own. Find 38 different Backyard cards and Outer Space opens up.

Broke? Somebody will always give you a pack.
