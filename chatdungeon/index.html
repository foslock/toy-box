Dungeon

You crawl the dungeon. Chat is the dungeon master.

Chat spends a shared jar of mischief to spawn monsters (with their names on), set traps, curse you, bless you, and vote on every door. Five floors. One Ban Ogre.

First time? A one-minute warm-up comes first.
Reads a channel's chat anonymously. Nothing is posted and there's no login. Viewers type !spawn goblin, !trap, !curse, !bless, and !left / !right at the doors.

How to play

What chat can type

!spawn bat2 mischief. Fast, fragile, flappy. !spawn goblin3. Winds up, then lunges. Sidestep it. !spawn slime3. Hops. Splits in two when it pops. !spawn skeleton4. Keeps its distance and shoots. Swat the arrows. !trap3. Spikes where you're standing in a second. !curse6. Fog, buttered floor, or backwards controls. !bless5. Heals a heart, or gives a shield if you're full. !left !rightFree. Votes on the next door.

Doors stay locked while anything's alive, chat's monsters included. Every kill puts a little mischief in the jar.
