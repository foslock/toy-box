This farm

A different farm

Every farm number digs up different dirt: other layers, rocks, lost things and food.

How it works

Put ants in by tapping the dirt (they dig themselves a pocket) or the top of it. Ants find food, dig to it and carry it home to the pantry. What's in the pantry is what you spend, and what your ants eat.

Workers
Fetch food; help dig when there's nothing to fetch.
Diggers
Dig tunnels and rooms three times as fast.
Scouts
Wander far and sniff out buried food.
Queen
Lays eggs that hatch into workers, when there's food to spare.
Nurses
Raise the queen's brood twice as fast.
Officers
Ants near her work faster, and she takes your orders to dig.

Food pockets run out. The sugar drip and aphids never do, but each only makes so much, so the farm can feed only so many ants. Too many, and the hungriest starve. Buy more aphids to feed more.

Drag to turn the farm, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to slide it. Keys: Space pause, 1-3 speed, H face the farm, Esc put down what you're holding.

Your farm is kept in this browser and rests while the page is closed.
